Challenges

Equipment Damage

Sometimes, your equipment can just give up on you. Whether that be during setup, or even during the catch itself, everything from the fishing line to the rod itself can be suspect to breaking. Lures and baits will be lost forever to the waters.
However, we must simply stand up again, buy new equipment, and make sure to be more careful the next day.

Bad Weather

Sometimes when we want to go out to fish, the weather will simply put a halt to our plans. But this is not a cause for worry. When the skys are gloomy, it becomes the perfect time to stay at home and prepare yourself. Maybe rewind your fishing line, prepare your lures, or even watch a tutorial!


Caught Nothing? Try again!

Fishing, like much of life, rewards you when you pay forward with patience. Even if you are at the best place and time, with the best equipment, catching a fish is never guarenteed. But everyone keeps casting their rod again, because everyone hopes that eventually, it will be their turn to get the catch of the day.
